## Metadata

*One block at the top of the proposal, six fields, no prose.*

- **ACP number:** the next free four-digit number, zero padded, matching the file name.
- **Title:** the name of the extension, as it will appear in the index.
- **Author:** the person proposing the extension, and the seat they hold.
- **Status:** `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected` or `superseded`.
- **Target version:** the minor version this extension would ship in, for example 0.4.
- **Date:** the date the proposal was opened, as YYYY-MM-DD, or as YYYY-MM where only the month is on record.
