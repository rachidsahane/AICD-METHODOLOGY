# Trademark policy

This document explains how the name "AICD" and the phrase "Artificial Intelligence Centered Development" may be used, and how that differs from the license on the methodology text.

## What the name covers

The name AICD, and its long form Artificial Intelligence Centered Development, identify this methodology as published in this repository: the document at `src/aicd.html`, its released versions, and the extensions adopted here.

The methodology text is free to use, share and adapt under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The name is not part of that license. Copyright and naming are separate questions, so permission to reuse the text is not permission to apply the name to something else.

## Permitted without asking

No permission, notice or fee is required for any of the following.

- Saying that a product, team, tool, service or course uses, implements, teaches or is compatible with AICD.
- Referring to AICD in articles, posts, talks, training material, standards work and academic work, including quoting and citing the document.
- Naming an adopted Change Proposal, or a profile, as part of AICD, once it has been merged into this repository.
- Stating plain facts about your own practice, for example "our verification cycle follows AICD" or "we run AICD 0.3".

## Not permitted

- Calling a modified or derived methodology AICD, or presenting it as AICD. Once you change the core, what you have is a derivative and it needs its own name.
- Using the name, or a name confusingly close to it, as the name of a product, company, service, account or domain in a way that implies your offering is the official methodology or is endorsed by the author.
- Using the name in a way that misleads about what is and is not part of the methodology, for example presenting an unadopted extension, profile, template set or tool as part of AICD, or implying that a certification or accreditation exists when none does.

## How to describe a derivative

Describe the relationship rather than claiming the name. These forms are correct and need no permission:

- "based on AICD"
- "an adaptation of AICD"
- "an AICD profile for X (not yet adopted)"

Put your own name first and keep the reference to AICD in the supporting text, so a reader can tell at a glance whose work they are looking at.

If you want an extension to become part of AICD rather than a derivative of it, submit an AICD Change Proposal, as described in the methodology and in [proposals/README.md](proposals/README.md). Once it is adopted and merged here, it is part of AICD and can be named as such.
